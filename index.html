Questions people ask before they call

How much does the first consultation cost?

Nothing. The first 45-minute consultation is free and there is no obligation to go further. If you decide to work with us, we tell you exactly how we are paid before you commit to anything.

How much do I need to retire in Singapore?

It depends on the income you want, when you stop working and what CPF LIFE will pay you. A common starting point is to work out the monthly income you want in today's dollars, subtract your expected CPF LIFE payout, and plan for savings that can cover the difference until at least age 90. Our free retirement gap calculator gives you a first estimate in two minutes.

Is CPF LIFE enough to retire on?

For most people, CPF LIFE covers basic needs but not the lifestyle they are used to. It is a strong foundation because it pays for life, so we plan your other savings and investments around it rather than instead of it.

Do I need a lot of money to work with a financial planner?

No. Many clients come to us in their twenties and thirties with modest savings. Starting early matters more than starting big, because time does most of the work.

What happens in the first meeting?

We talk about your goals, what you have today and what worries you. You leave with a clear picture of where you stand and two or three next steps, whether or not you work with us.

How do you keep my information safe?

We only ask for what we need to reply to you, we never sell your details, and you can ask us to delete them at any time. Your information is handled in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).
